Figures are for the 61 aircraft synthetic airline in the prototype.

What it does

1Predict

Scores every departure for late risk two hours ahead, using a model trained on real flights.

2Simulate

Tests thousands of recovery plans in under a second and shows the cost of each.

3Explain and approve

Claude explains the best plan and drafts passenger notices. A person approves every plan.

How it is built

  • Simulator in JavaScript with 8 automated tests
  • Delay model in Python on 6.3 million real flights, with weather
  • Fuel layer on US EIA spot prices for October 6, 2026
  • Claude as the advisor, with a person always approving
  • Code, data pipeline and model card are public on GitHub
Why it matters for clients. The airline is the example. The same pattern of predict, simulate, explain and approve carries to energy, healthcare, government and telecom. A first step is one hub for 26 weeks, ending in a saving finance can sign off. These are estimates from a simulated airline, and the pilot replaces them with a measured number.
